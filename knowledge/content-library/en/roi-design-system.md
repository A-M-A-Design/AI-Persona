# How do you measure the ROI of a Design System?
Article written by Arthur Mathon (LinkedIn). Tags: Business, DesignSystem.

We will never stop evangelising the practice of DesignOps and the use of Design Systems, both of which help amplify the value of design. Once fully integrated, design systems become a genuine lever for structural, organisational and strategic transformation. But how do you measure their return on investment?

As a DesignOps practitioner, you will quickly realise that measuring the impact of your work matters a great deal — to your company and to your manager alike. Why does it matter so much? Because the discipline is still being defined, and the haze surrounding the profession and its usefulness persists. Ten years ago, designers had to summon considerable inventiveness and energy to justify and legitimise their place in industry. The same is true today for DesignOps: we have to demonstrate the real contribution our work makes to organisations. In this article I will try to pass on a few keys for measuring the ROI of your design systems.

## What is ROI?

Return on investment is a performance metric used to analyse the effectiveness of an investment, whether that investment is a person or a product.

For a design system, it might take the form of increased productivity, a better customer experience or a faster time to market. It makes your main objectives tangible — namely growing revenue and reducing costs.

## What are the qualitative returns on investment?

- Design systems help teams meet delivery deadlines and hit quality targets, by introducing a language that every team in your product's value chain can understand, whether they are designers, front-end developers, product owners and so on.
- They shorten your time to market by optimising and automating your business processes.
- They reduce the risk of design and implementation errors, because teams rely on a component library and guidelines that have already been proven.
- Styles and tokens let your digital services evolve without demanding the repetitive, laborious work that is so often prone to human error.
- They help you communicate your corporate identity more effectively.
- They bring every design decision together in a single file, becoming a reliable source of truth for all your colleagues.

## What are the quantitative returns on investment?

- A complete design system, fully integrated into how the company operates, can increase your productivity by up to 25%.
- You will need less custom CSS in your development work.
- Implementation will require fewer colour properties and CSS declarations.
- The more designers and developers use your design system, the fewer feature-specific CSS properties you will need for any single feature.

## Which KPIs should you track to measure the effectiveness of your design system's ROI?

## Team effectiveness

- The time your teams need to build a new product using your design system.

## Speed to market

- The time your teams need to build and test prototypes.

## Effect on the code

- The amount of code developers change with each release.
- The number of distinct CSS classes needed to build your products.

## Effect on design work

- The number of components and styles your teams build from scratch.
- The number of contributors to, and visitors of, your design system.
- The number of projects that make use of your design system.
